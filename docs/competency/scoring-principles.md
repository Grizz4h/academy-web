# Competency Scoring Principles

No scoring formula is implemented in Phase 1. These constraints govern the future engine.

## Training is not evidence

A training weight says what a drill practices. An evidence weight says what actual user performance may support. A high training weight never implies a high evidence weight. Drill completion alone creates neither positive evidence nor competence.

## XP is not competence

XP measures activity, progress and gamification. Competence is inferred from assessed user performance. XP/rewards and competency remain separate domains.

## Evidence levels

1. Fundamental recognition evidence
2. Simple application or connection
3. Complex situational application
4. Systemic or integrative evidence
5. Analytical transfer

The highest available evidence level must later limit how strong a score claim can become. Strong Level-1 performance alone cannot justify a 90+ score.

## Confidence, breadth and farming protection

A score requires a separate confidence statement. Repeating one drill may increase evidence count and support consistency, but must not arbitrarily maximize breadth, confidence or score. Breadth must depend on distinct drills, tracks and situations.

## AI boundary

AI may later assess one evidence item, including quality, specificity, evidence alignment and unsupported claims. It may never directly assign the user's final competency score: **AI evaluates evidence. RinQ computes competence.**
